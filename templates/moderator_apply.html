About the Community Moderator Role

Community Moderators are trusted student leaders appointed to guide faculty-specific academic discussions. Moderators are faculty-scoped: upon administrative endorsement, your moderation actions will oversee questions, solutions, and shared course materials originating strictly within your assigned faculty.

🔍 Faculty Scope: Review reported questions, answers, and study resources in your faculty queue.
⚖️ Academic Integrity: Resolve disputes constructively and uphold CodeNest academic honesty standards.
🏅 Verified Leadership: Receive the official Community Moderator badge across MMU discussions.
