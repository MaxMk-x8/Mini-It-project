1. PROJECT CONCEPT

Codenest is a Python Flask web application for university students to share study notes, ask and
answer academic/programming questions, and build a student community. It has three pillars:

- Study Notes & Resources — upload, browse, and download course material.
- Student Q&A — ask and answer academic/programming questions.
- Community & Moderation — posts, reports, and a student-run moderation layer.

Track: Web Application, using Python/Flask, satisfying CLO1 (build a solution to a simple IT problem
using programming tools).

Per our supervisor's direction, Codenest is organised into faculty-scoped spaces (Section 2).


2. FACULTY STRUCTURE

The Q&A and Resource Hub pillars are organised by faculty. Two faculties for this phase: FCI
(Faculty of Computer Information) and FOM (Faculty of Management). Single shared codebase and
database, with faculty as one extra column on the resource and question tables, above the existing
subject/course taxonomy (e.g. FCI -> Computer Science -> Data Structures, or FOM -> Marketing ->
Consumer Behaviour).

- Users select their faculty at registration; it is stored on their profile and pre-selects their
  default faculty tab.
- Browsing is shared: an FCI/FOM filter sits above both the Q&A list and the Resource Hub. Posting
  always tags to one faculty.
- Admin stays platform-wide, covering both FCI and FOM.
- Community Moderators are faculty-scoped: an FCI moderator reviews/removes only FCI-tagged reports,
  an FOM moderator only FOM ones, since a moderator outside their own faculty can't judge whether
  flagged academic content is appropriate.
- Professor answer-priority applies within their own faculty's Q&A.

Community posts remain platform-wide for this phase.


3. USER TYPES

Student
Registration requires an email ending in @student.mmu.edu.my; the Student role is auto-assigned on
match.
- Register, log in/out, and manage a profile
- Upload and download study notes
- Ask and answer questions
- Create community posts
- Report inappropriate content
- Apply to become a Community Moderator

Professor
Registration using the staff domain @mmu.edu.my auto-assigns the Professor role. Admin can correct a
mistaken registration.
- Everything a Student can do
- A badge shown beside their name on profile, posts, and answers
- Answers given display priority, sorted above other answers on a question

Community Moderator
Students selected by Admin from a pool of applicants; do not have full administrative access.
Faculty-scoped: an approved moderator's faculty (from their application form) becomes their
moderation scope, so an FCI moderator only handles FCI reports and an FOM moderator only FOM ones.
Applicants apply via a form (Section 4, Pritiv) that collects student ID/matric number and faculty,
verified against their @student.mmu.edu.my account.
- Review reported posts, questions, and resources within their own faculty
- Remove inappropriate content within their own faculty
- Manage discussions and basic community disputes
- A coloured badge shown beside their name on profile, posts, and answers (distinct colour from the
  Professor badge)

Admin
- Manage users and assign/remove Community Moderator roles
- Approve or reject Community Moderator applications
- Remove inappropriate content and manage resources platform-wide
- View basic platform statistics
